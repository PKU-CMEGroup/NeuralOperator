The folder historically labeled forward_300 is the CPG supersonic-bump bundle:
300 training trajectories, 20 test trajectories, 80 HDF5 frames, and roughly
19k--23k graph nodes per trajectory.

- [CPG_EULER_DATASET_CONTRACT.md](CPG_EULER_DATASET_CONTRACT.md) defines the

- [BUMP_300_DATASET_AUDIT.md](BUMP_300_DATASET_AUDIT.md) records raw-bundle and

- [CPGGNSPDES_REFERENCE_AUDIT.md](CPGGNSPDES_REFERENCE_AUDIT.md) records the
  bounded public-reference audit.
- [SECTION_1_2_CORRECTED_BASELINES.md](SECTION_1_2_CORRECTED_BASELINES.md)
  preserves corrected historical 1D labels and results.

The bump HDF5 does not expose validated finite-volume control volumes, oriented
physical faces, measures, normals, or accepted-substep impulses. Equal-node or
reconstructed-weight totals are diagnostic proxies, not physical conservation.

The separate Mach-1.1 shock-vortex family has audited finite-volume geometry,
boundary accounting, and cumulative accepted-substep impulses. Those fields
support physical outcome diagnostics only on that frozen solver and data
contract. State-residual PCNO recurrence is not conservative by construction.

Its family-local cell types are `0=interior`, `1=touches y-symmetry`,
`2=touches x-extrapolation`, and `3=touches both`, with the combined corner
category taking precedence. These meanings are not interchangeable with the
bump vertex labels.

D068--D069 close the current node-type mechanism question. Frozen replacements
show that the maintained dynamic and bump checkpoints use their own family-local
semantics, with wall labels decisive in the tested native bump geometries.
A matched three-seed dynamic study does not support a training benefit from four
permanently zero channels. This is a boundary-representation diagnosis under a
frozen physical boundary policy, not a boundary-condition improvement or an
optimal-encoding claim. See the compact tracker for exact artifact pointers.

The owner-authorized D072 fixed-physical-width boundary-field ladder is
complete and not promoted. Its arms were no field, one geometry-only union
collar, and separate family-local semantic collars under exact no-boundary
matched initialization. Dynamic FV retained `model_all_nodes`; bump retained
`causal_nodal_physical`. Across three BF16 seeds, G1 worsens dynamic H30 and
S1's 1.05% mean reduction misses the registered promotion gate; neither field
arm improves bump completion or common-case H79.
One dynamic FP32 seed causally uses the learned fields, but the field arms do
not clear N0-relative structure controls and their ranking is precision
sensitive. The first ladder therefore supports bounded descriptor construction
and one-seed field-use evidence, not an optimal encoding, boundary-condition
improvement, resolution/geometry/rotation generalization, conservation, or
operator convergence. D084 later supplies a separate checkpoint-bound D082 bump
intervention/continuation package; it does not retroactively promote D072.
D072 resolution/rotation stress tests did not run; exact evidence and re-entry
requirements are in the compact tracker.

D084 is complete and closed on all 30 open bump validation cases. All 360
six-variant/two-repeat H79 continuations remain finite and avoid every registered
global explosion event, although 98 rows become inadmissible and some later show
large local growth. Exact model and output-policy repairs occur, but are neither
universal nor durable. Zeroing the D082 inflow field improves strict completion
from 19/30 to 23/30 at nearly unchanged mean strict error while retaining large
local excursions; other field removals expose different completion/error
tradeoffs. These frozen-checkpoint interventions show field use, not a causal
effect of inadmissibility, a general stabilizer, or a promotable D082/D072
method. Six canonical case-23/54/128 MP4s retain all 80 frames with full-node
smooth fields, fixed reference-only scales, and no mesh or ordinary boundary
overlays.

The separate D083 frozen-D041 bump experiment is now complete. Its G1a arm is
proxy-mass query-graph resampling under severe compression, not PDE resolution
transfer. Its G1b arm transported Fourier modes with the domain and is retained
only as an analytic covariance negative control. D085 is the distinct
fixed-checkpoint-Fourier 90-degree transformed-input test requested by the
owner; it keeps the original physical modes and `[6,2]` periods fixed while
rotated raw coordinates and state enter PCNO directly. Its exact-source H2
and terminal 30-case H79 package pass all evaluator gates, but every rotated
arm fails at the first finite proposal. The aligned call-1 defect is therefore
a same-input representation failure before recurrence, and its raw physical
RMS is material. Fixed-world Fourier representation worsens D083's
transported-basis control in all cases while leaving a large non-Fourier
remainder. This is expected for a one-orientation checkpoint with absolute
coordinates, fixed world-axis Fourier phases, componentwise normalization, and
unconstrained learned x/y gradient mixing rather than enforced rotation
covariance. D085 is an unseen-orientation stress of retained cases, not an
unseen-case or Fourier-only test. D086 completes the bounded visualization
follow-up for cases `172/58/187`: all case/type/repeat continuations are invalid
from call 1 but finite through H79, and six verified shared-scale movies show
broad residual and pressure failure across the rotated domain. Late invalid
fields are repeat-sensitive. These are explicitly labeled failure
continuations, not accepted rollouts. Neither experiment is a boundary-condition
intervention, independent rotated PDE solve, broad geometry-generalization
result, stability result, or physical-conservation claim.

D070C and D073-A separately narrow the dynamic resolution defect to the
differential-geometry pathway. Under same-hidden inputs, replacing the
mesh-local layer-3 graph-ball radius by the training-grid physical width reduces
every registered adjacent-pair stratum and passes native relevance. The newer
D074 direction makes native `250x100` persistent residual correction the first
practical target and freezes transfer-to-native, rollout, and transfer-back as
the comparator for direct off-grid methods. D071's local filter is not retained
because it worsens native residual and local-band error. D073-B remains useful
only after it is compared against the best raw or corrected transfer-native
pipeline. This is not yet a corrected-rollout, resolution-invariance, or bump-
transfer claim; exact contracts and metrics live in the compact tracker.
The D074-A evaluator and fixed-scale renderer are CPU-qualified and independently
reviewed. Original-source and post-validator dynamic H2 contract smokes complete
without scientific interpretation; exact regenerated `250x100` geometry and two
post-validator repeats close the registered H30 prerequisites. The repaired
dynamic H30 run `d074_dynamic_h30_r2` completes under source
`892745de...86e8`; all 23 declared result hashes pass. Every nonzero
candidate/case pair improves the H30 endpoint, but every candidate violates at
least one frozen no-harm gate, so the selector correctly retains `zero` and no
correction promotes. The closest complete-case arm, `rank8_gain0p25`, passes
323/324 case-metric rows and misses only the `sv_e10_y00` energy-integral RMS
gate (`1.09823` versus `1.05`). This supports a useful persistent correction
direction, not a safe corrected-rollout claim. The six evaluation cases therefore
replay the raw baseline exactly. Bump remains closed behind its exact D041 replay
binding. Exact source identities, tolerances, metrics, and artifacts are recorded
in the compact tracker.

D075--D079 refine that persistent direction: deterministic D078 promotes a
strength-grouped rank-8 controller on the adaptive open dynamic population, and
D079 reproduces every scientific payload byte-for-byte in a fresh process.
D080 then tests a separate causal local channel. Its frozen-direction audit
finds shock-local headroom, but the recurrent H30 pilot promotes no always-on
local arm. Shock filtering helps early and reduces smooth high-pass error, then
reverses and worsens every shock endpoint; the vortex effect changes sign with
placement and violates a global-integral control. The fixed-scale D080 analysis
package retains componentwise residual, prediction, defect, local correction,
cumulative-defect, and signed-growth animations. These are native `250x100`,
dynamic-only adaptive open-validation results under unchanged
`model_all_nodes`; they do not establish conservation, boundary-condition
improvement, bump transfer, sealed performance, or independent confirmation.

D081 closes the smallest timing follow-up on the same six open dynamic cases.
Calls 1--20 outperform always-on, calls 11--30, and a dose-matched control on
the registered timing comparisons, but improve the persistent endpoint in only
`3/6` cases. Early-versus-persistent median endpoint/residual/cumulative/shock
ratios are `0.999971/0.993392/0.999677/0.997485`; no arm promotes. Preserve this
as a small placement-dependent timing signal, not a safe corrected rollout.

## Source-Snapshot And Recovery Notes

New PCNO runs use source-snapshot schema v5. Schemas v3--v5 each bind their
schema-specific executable/scientific source inventory for continuation. The
active decision and experiment index are copied and hashed separately as
provenance, so later documentation edits do not invalidate continuation.

Historical v2 snapshots retain their original strict equality semantics,
including the then-current decision and tracker bytes. They are not silently
reinterpreted as v5. Historical v3 and v4 snapshots likewise retain their
registered source inventories rather than being expanded to the v5 set.

Key recovery anchors:

- ae1f402: D063 evidence record;
- e2070f6: reusable PCNO infrastructure and the full active documentation
  immediately before documentation compaction;
- ce5d6a2: pre-infrastructure-cleanup source and retired diagnostic entry
  points; and
- 31e5765: earlier expanded documentation provenance.

The compact index records experiment-specific source and artifact pointers.
Its post-archive D064--D069 section is the source of truth for the accepted
residual-structure, node-type intervention, and zero-channel training summaries,
repeatability qualifications, and final visual bundles; generated arrays and
media remain under ignored artifact storage.
Core pcno/, baselines/, and unrelated examples remain outside this
branch-specific inventory.
