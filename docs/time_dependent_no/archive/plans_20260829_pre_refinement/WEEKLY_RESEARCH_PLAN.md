## Goal For Day 14

Deliver a serious first submittable manuscript for mentor review with:

- the two claims and scope aligned throughout;
- complete qualified theory and terminology;
- a concise, verified literature taxonomy covering the representative
  corrective-mechanism families;
- a compact exact/learned ODE laboratory as supporting evidence;
- a verified primary-PDE case study that diagnoses the problem, freezes
  intervention predictions, and compares the selected embedded/training-time
  and explicit mechanisms under one fixed PCNO backbone, plus the hybrid if its
  contract and audit close;
- complete main figures, tables, statistics, cost accounting, and limitations;
  and
- a cleanly built, inspected PDF and claim/provenance checklist.

The deadline does not justify fabricated numbers, an unqualified PDE, a new
architecture sweep, or a sealed/test opening. An ODE-only draft or an explicit
primary-PDE evidence gap does not meet this Day-14 completion gate. A second
PDE remains optional until the primary case study is complete.

## Day-2/3 Primary-PDE Decision

The selected PDE proceeds only if all are available:

- qualified reference and fresh population;
- state/restart/boundary closure;
- solver error below interpreted model differences;
- fixed PCNO model mesh and resource envelope;
- open prospective population whose long horizons remain unopened;
- common query bank and frozen diagnostics; and
- complete source/data/evaluator manifest plan.

Current M1 supplies only qualified finite-grid reference packets. Its old model
contract uses residual FNO, not the newly fixed PCNO, and its R2-POP attempts
produced no scientific packet. Another retry or Q2 is not automatic. REALM is
not an active candidate because the required solver is unavailable.

No exact PDE is selected by this calendar. The owner chooses it after reviewing
the experiment design. If the first candidate fails a hard readiness condition,
select a qualified replacement by Day 3. If none is available, report that the
Day-14 serious-submittable gate cannot be met and revise scope or timing with
the owner; do not relabel an ODE-only draft as complete.
