# Scope And Parked Work

Updated: 2026-09-16

[PROJECT_PLAN.md](PROJECT_PLAN.md) owns the current objective. This page prevents
old queues from becoming current commitments. Parking preserves evidence and
code; it neither rejects a whole method family nor authorizes reactivation.
Exact prior dispositions and re-entry recipes are retained in the
[pre-reset ledger](archive/pre_predictive_reset_20260916a/PARKED_EXPERIMENTS.md).

## Present Selection Boundary

Documentation planning is the only work now authorized. The main PDE case,
quantitative prediction, comparison and deadline remain to be agreed.
Kolmogorov is the leading candidate; its current recipe is not the final plan.
Existing ODE, NACA and Bump results are available for selective paper use.

| Item | Current role | Reason to select additional work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prepared Kolmogorov Gaussian three-arm adaptation | Reusable implementation and bank; held pending scientific selection. | It answers the selected target/response question at relevant times, amplitudes and directions. |
| Local forcing/response forecast | Candidate predictive bridge, not a validated diagnostic. | A bounded calibration/validity test can support the chosen application decision. |
| New corrector, spectral damping or recovery/relabeling buffer | Candidate ideas after diagnosis, not selected methods. | A measured tradeoff motivates a simple intervention and a falsifiable quantitative prediction. |
| Existing ODE laboratories | Completed supporting illustrations. | A specific paper-critical gap cannot be addressed by existing evidence. |
| NACA and Bump follow-up | Optional supporting evidence; no automatic rerun. | One distinct claim needs a bounded missing measurement. |
| Additional regime or PDE | Optional contrast. | It tests a stated limit not answered by the main case; no mandatory second PDE. |
| More seeds, architectures or method variants | Not an inherited matrix. | The uncertainty or claim breadth requires that specific addition. |

The representative-mechanism objective remains. It does not require every
family on every PDE, nor a universal ranking. Select the smallest comparisons
that distinguish actual inputs, targets, deployment maps and information costs.

## Outside The Present Paper Programme

- Online OOD detection and exogenous-OOD generalization.
- Broad architecture/PDE benchmarks, exhaustive corrector reproduction and
  unrelated RL/controller or simulator-acquisition programmes.
- Automatic continuation of D094, W26, P0/P1, M1/M2 or REALM queues.
- New infrastructure frameworks, source relocations or cleanup campaigns
  without a concrete need in the selected task.

Their scientific findings, failed attempts and reusable components remain
historical evidence. Closed gates retain the scope of the exact tested attempt;
they are not universal method prohibitions. Reuse does not reopen a frozen
population or authorize a changed scientific recipe.

## Diagnostic And Access Boundaries

Offline reference proximity, empirical PCA, spectral response and solver defect
can inform analysis. They are not automatically true manifold coordinates,
online OOD detectors, or uniform stability certificates. A deployed correction
requires its own specified map and information cost.

Protected prospective/test populations remain closed until the applicable
named owner decision. Private context, datasets, credentials and unpublished
material retain their standing access boundaries.

Search [MECHANISTIC_DIAGNOSTIC_TRACKER.md](MECHANISTIC_DIAGNOSTIC_TRACKER.md) and
the dated [result ledger](EXPERIMENT_TRACKER.md) by exact run ID for evidence.
Preserve original source/data/evaluator bindings. A changed recipe takes a new
identity; a new plan does not rewrite historical outcomes.
