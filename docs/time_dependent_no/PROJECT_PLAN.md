## Objective

Deliver a rigorous, intuitive, and empirically testable framework for why
clean one-step accuracy can fail to predict autoregressive rollout quality,
and for choosing corrective mechanisms for long-horizon time-dependent PDE
prediction from in-distribution initial conditions.

The paper succeeds only if it combines:

1. a qualified theoretical account of the clean-trace/deployment-response gap;
2. a non-tautological measurement framework;
3. a cutoff-bounded field taxonomy with representative empirical methods;
4. a mandatory end-to-end fixed-PCNO case study on one qualified complex PDE;
   and
5. a serious manuscript whose claims match verified evidence.

## Thesis And Two Claims

Let `Phi_n` be the trusted transition and `Psi_n` the complete deployed learned
transition. Clean supervision constrains `Psi_n` on reference states, whereas
self-composition evaluates it on states generated by earlier predictions.
Long-horizon control therefore depends on forcing, response, and the region
visited by the deployed trajectory.

A **corrective mechanism** is any training, structural, numerical, or deployed
intervention that retains the rollout in a controlled region, enlarges the
region of accurate response, restricts the response there, or adds feedback.
An **operational corrector** is a separately attributable deployed map or
kernel with a fixed interface and identity ablation. Claims attach to `Psi`;
the factorization `Psi = C o F` is non-unique.

The empirical story has two non-substitutable axes. Clean one-step error
measures fidelity of the learned map on clean reference inputs--the formal
object behind the tangent/path intuition--but does not generally identify its
response to displaced inputs. A corrective mechanism supplies or improves the
transverse retention/recovery property of the complete deployed map. Accurate
rollout requires both: good on-reference dynamics with poor correction can
drift, while strong retention with poor tangent/phase dynamics can remain
stable but wrong. The on-reference/tangent observation is established
background; the paper contribution is the joint framework, diagnostics,
taxonomy, and prospective selection of corrective mechanisms.

| Claim | Minimum convincing evidence | Drop condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **C1: conditional response non-identifiability.** In a sufficiently rich clean-data equivalence class, clean one-step supervision does not identify deployment-relevant off-trace response. Uniform control needs equivalence-breaking information or a justified structural restriction. | Exact pathwise identity, scalar ranking reversal, one central clean-trace theorem, a qualified tube/path recurrence, and explicit counterexamples. | The hypothesis/world class or geometry assumptions cannot be stated precisely, or the text claims necessity when structure already identifies the response. |
| **C2: prospective empirical value.** For a fixed PCNO backbone, frozen response, forcing, and drift diagnostics predict long-rollout ranking reversals and regime-dependent intervention value beyond clean one-step error. | A qualified primary PDE, frozen common bank, pre-reveal predictions, representative embedded and operational mechanisms, three paired seeds where training is stochastic, and a held-out reveal with mediator and no-harm checks. | Diagnostics are only retrospective, add no held-out predictive value, or the intervention changes rollout without the declared mediator. |

The field study, ODE laboratory, and any proposed hybrid support C1 or C2.
They are not additional headline contributions.

## Non-Tautological Measurement Contract

The paper keeps three objects separate:

| Object | Definition | Permitted role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reference geometry or tube | Defined independently of the evaluated model from clean reference data and declared physical structure. | Describes data support or a state-only proxy for endogenous drift. |
| Solver-relative fidelity | `D_n^Psi(x) = Psi_n(x) - Phi_n(x)` and trusted response measurements at qualified queries. | Offline scientific diagnosis and mechanism validation only. It does not define ID, OOD, or the tube. |
| Deployed transition | The fixed `Psi_n` and only the information declared available at inference. | Produces the online rollout. The core experiment makes no online `Phi_n` query. |

`ID` refers to the declared initial-condition and clean-data law. A large
solver-relative defect is called a trusted-solver-relative response failure, not
OOD. Near-reference and low-defect states are allowed to disagree; those
disagreements are important falsifiers.

The empirical contribution is prospective prediction, not the observation
that a model is inaccurate where a solver later reports a large defect.
